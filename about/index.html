About WordTally

WordTally is a single-purpose writing tool: a live word, character, and sentence counter with reading time, speaking time, and keyword density, all in one clean page.

We built it deliberately small. Rather than bundling dozens of unrelated utilities into one bloated site, WordTally does one job and does it well — count and analyze your text instantly, with no clutter and no distractions.

Everything runs entirely in your browser. There is no upload, no account, and no server processing your text. When you close the tab, nothing you typed persists anywhere.

Reading time is calculated at 200 words per minute and speaking time at 130 words per minute — both commonly used averages for adult reading and presentation pacing. Keyword density highlights your top 5 most-used meaningful words, filtering out common stopwords like “the”, “and”, and “is” so you can see what your writing actually emphasizes.

WordTally is free to use, with no signup required.
