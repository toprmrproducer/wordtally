Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

Your text stays on your device

WordTally performs all word, character, sentence, and keyword counting directly in your browser using JavaScript. The text you type or paste into the tool is never transmitted to our servers, never stored, and never seen by us. Closing or refreshing the tab clears it completely.

Analytics

We may use privacy-respecting analytics to understand aggregate traffic (e.g. page views, general location, device type) so we can improve the site. This data is not tied to the content you type into the counter.

Advertising

This site may display advertisements served by third-party ad networks, such as Google AdSense. These networks may use cookies or similar technologies to serve relevant ads based on your visit to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising through your ad network's settings or through your browser's cookie controls.

Cookies

We do not use cookies to store or identify the text you enter. Cookies set by advertising or analytics providers are governed by their own privacy policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of WordTally after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please reach out through the contact details listed on our About page.
