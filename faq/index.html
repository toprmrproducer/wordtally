Frequently asked questions

Is WordTally free to use?

Yes. WordTally is completely free, with no signup, no account, and no usage limits.

Does my text get uploaded anywhere?

No. All counting happens locally in your browser using JavaScript. Your text is never sent to a server.

How is reading time calculated?

Reading time is estimated at 200 words per minute, a commonly used average adult silent-reading speed. Speaking time is estimated at 130 words per minute, a typical spoken presentation pace.

What is the Flesch reading-ease score?

It is a standard readability formula that rates text from 0 (very difficult) to 100 (very easy) based on average sentence length and syllables per word. We also show an approximate Flesch-Kincaid grade level. Both are estimates computed live in your browser.

What counts as a "sentence"?

WordTally counts sentence-ending punctuation (periods, question marks, and exclamation points) to estimate sentence count. Abbreviations or unusual punctuation may occasionally affect accuracy slightly.

What is keyword density?

Keyword density shows your top 10 most-repeated meaningful words as a percentage of your total countable words, excluding common stopwords like "the", "a", and "is".

Does WordTally work on mobile?

Yes. WordTally is fully responsive and works on phones, tablets, and desktop browsers.

Is there a word or character limit?

No hard limit is enforced. Since everything runs in your browser, very large documents may feel slightly slower depending on your device, but typical documents, essays, and articles work instantly.
